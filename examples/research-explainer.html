What does a learning trace leave out?

A system records an action. A person gives that action context. The research question lives in the relationship between them.

Illustrative research question and proposed method. No study has been conducted and no findings are claimed here.

Experience → interpretation → record

A proposed way to investigate

Keep the account beside the event.

Compare a participant's explanation with the trace, then document where they agree and where the trace is incomplete.

Question

How do participants describe returning to a learning resource, and which parts of those accounts appear in the recorded events?

Method sketch

Use consented accounts and a defined set of events. Compare explanations case by case. Record alternative interpretations before grouping the observations.

Limitation

An account is also partial. Agreement between an account and a trace would not, by itself, show that an intervention caused learning.
