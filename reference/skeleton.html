Skeleton

Placeholder text lines, circles and blocks that hold a layout's shape while content loads; they pulse in place under reduced motion.

Guidance

Use skeletons when you know the shape of what's coming (a card, a list, a profile) and it takes longer than ~300ms. They prevent layout shift and feel calmer than a spinner. For an unknown or short wait, use a spinner.

Accessibility

  • Skeleton shapes are aria-hidden="true"; the loading region has aria-busy="true" and a visually hidden role="status" message.
  • Remove aria-busy (and the skeletons) in the same update that inserts the content.
  • Under prefers-reduced-motion the sweeping shimmer becomes a slow in-place pulse, so the placeholder never looks finished.
