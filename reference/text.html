Guidance

Size comes from the typography composites in tokens/base.css (.type-body-lg, .type-small, …); .mp-text adds the two things pages otherwise hand-code: which text color and how wide a line may run. It also removes the default paragraph margin, because spacing belongs to the surrounding Stack.

ToneTokenUse
(none)inheritsbody copy
muted--color-text-secondaryledes, descriptions
subtle--color-text-tertiarymetadata, timestamps
human--color-human-textwarm emphasis, overlines
digital--color-digital-texttechnical emphasis

Accessibility

  • Every tone is a gate-checked text token (≥ 4.5:1 on page and card surfaces in both modes).
  • data-measure keeps lines near 65 characters (WCAG 1.4.8 guidance for readable text).
