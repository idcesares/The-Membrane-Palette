## Approaches

Editorial drawings emphasize gesture and annotation. Structural constructions emphasize frames, routes and alignment. The selected combination joins these qualities through one family of organic traces, permeable boundaries and precise frames. RSE informs the relationship between motif, asset and composition; its cube geometry and institutional expression are not reused.

## Craft and constraints

Each focal asset should work as a picture before being used as a background. The organic side uses deliberately drawn, repeatable curves, not random jitter; the structural side uses clear alignments and a consistent stroke hierarchy. Distinguish warm/cool roles with geometry and nearby language as well as color. Two register accents plus neutral linework/surfaces form the default; larger explanatory scenes may add semantic colors when their meaning requires it. Never reduce readable labels or essential diagram strokes to atmospheric opacity.

Use native HTML and existing `mp-*` components with authored `composition-*` styles. Static specimens need no JavaScript, theme toggles or runtime dependency. Use matching light/dark SVG variants with native picture sources, and an explicit dark variant within the dark presentation specimen. Assets have intrinsic titles/descriptions for standalone use; HTML uses empty alternatives for decorative art and a nearby text equivalent for meaningful diagrams. No motion is needed. All demonstration copy is illustrative, without personal achievement or study claims.
